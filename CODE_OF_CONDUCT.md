# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and safe.

Be welcoming, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good intent, and accept technical feedback gracefully. Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact shown on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
